Nalani West

Our Caregiver of the Month

For two and a half years, Nalani has been someone clients, families, and our scheduling team can count on.

Please join us in thanking her for the care she gives and the difference she makes.

This month, Nalani completed 67 shifts and 618 hours of care for six different clients. Her record: 100% on-time arrivals, 100% EVV compliance, and 100% task completion—with zero call-offs.

She also covered 25 weekend shifts, helping our clients receive the care they needed seven days a week. Nalani shows our values in action: Accountable for Results, Adaptable in Creating the Future, and Empowered to Act.

“Nalani West is an outstanding ambassador for Comfort Keepers. Her recent heroic act while caring for a client reflects her compassion, dedication, and commitment to those we serve. Nalani is always willing to step in when needed and consistently receives compliments for the care she provides. We are grateful for her hard work and proud to have her on our team.”

Angie Busby-Davis
Operations Manager
